# Code of conduct: TypeSafeAI Community

> **Unofficial community organization, not the official TypeSafe AI team.** Created by VC Moderator [@BunsDev](https://github.com/BunsDev).

We want this community to be a welcoming, curious, and useful place for developers, researchers, and anyone learning to build with TypeSafe AI and Jev. This code applies to every TypeSafeAI repository, issue, pull request, and discussion, and to anyone representing the community. A repository's own `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` takes precedence for that repository.

It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Do:

- be respectful, patient, and specific, especially with people who are new;
- critique ideas and results, not people;
- credit original authors and say where code, prompts, and data came from;
- be honest about what was measured, mocked, or simulated.

Do not:

- harass, insult, threaten, or demean anyone, or make sexualized comments or advances;
- attack people over identity, background, ability, or experience level;
- publish someone's private information, credentials, or data without permission;
- claim to speak for the official TypeSafe AI team, or present community work as official;
- spam issues or pull requests, including with unreviewed bulk or agent-generated content;
- retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization moderator, [@BunsDev](https://github.com/BunsDev), or to a maintainer of the affected repository through a private channel you have already verified. You can also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may restrict or remove participants who violate this code. Responses usually escalate from a private correction, to a warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban. Threats, doxxing, credential exposure, or severe harassment may go directly to a ban.
